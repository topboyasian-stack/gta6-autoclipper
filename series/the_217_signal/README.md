# THE 2:17 SIGNAL

Original animated serialized science-fiction mystery. This folder is the source of truth for the fictional world, cast, season arcs and recurring production design.

- [Series bible](series_bible.json): premise, world rules, character locks, tone, locations and provisional voice assignments.
- [Season 1 outline](season_01/outline.json): eight planned episodes and the season arc.
- [Character reference prompts](character_reference_prompts.md): separate character turnaround prompts.

## Production status

**Development / pre-production.** Character reference images have not yet been approved. Voice assignments are provisional auditions, not locked casting. Do not publish an episode until the character references and voice cast are auditioned and approved.

The main episode format is landscape 16:9, approximately 3–4 minutes for the pilot. Vertical Shorts are separate discovery edits, not substitutes for full episodes.

## Continuity

Only episodes that have passed final human review and been marked canonical may update the continuity ledger. Drafts must never silently rewrite established story facts.
